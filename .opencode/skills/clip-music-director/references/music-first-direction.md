# Music-first direction — standing crew contract

One lead: `film-director` is the music-video director and sole canonical/paid
coordinator, not a second director competing with a music director. Create a
coherent visual performance of this recording; narrative is optional. These
rules apply to all seven roles, not just the lead's taste in one production.

## Before the board is fixed

1. Map the actual recording. Keep measurements, supplied style/lyric intent,
   listening interpretations and user-reviewed events separately identified.
   Candidate BPM does not establish meter, bars or downbeat phase. Section labels,
   accents and lyric timestamps require evidence; unknown remains unknown.
2. Establish one conceit and visual world: palette/light/texture/optics, format,
   relevant people/places/states and two or three evolving motifs where useful.
   Use existing `brief.treatment`, `creative.arc`, `creative.look`, characters and
   bound scenes/states; no parallel world-bible database or mandatory reference
   sheet purchase. References are selective, consented and budgeted.
3. Have `music-editor` and `post-editor` contribute BEFORE storyboard lock and
   image generation. The director dispatches them sequentially or in bounded
   independent lanes; specialists never dispatch each other. They help design
   the sequence, not merely animate a fixed collection of attractive stills.
4. Write a sequence treatment inside existing brief/draft fields. For each passage:
   musical evidence/uncertainty; intended visual experience; sequence mechanics
   (scale, cuts, holds, panels, text, movement); contrast from its predecessor;
   and what is being withheld for later. Symbolic, performance, process and pure
   abstraction can have visual progression without invented character causality.

Exploratory concepts before music review are provisional and based on supplied
intent; do not present them as timed treatments or approve a music-bound board.

## Rhythm, motion and sequence logic

- Musical units inform timing only when verified. The canonical edit is still
  contiguous integer frames. Cuts may answer beats, phrases, vocals, silence,
  visual action or deliberate anticipation/counterpoint. No rule that every cut
  must hit a beat/downbeat, no universal one/two-frame advance, no energy→duration
  formula. Document the reason for a deliberate offbeat cut rather than fail it.
- User duration buckets are useful proposals, not clocks to fill. A frantic
  passage may earn a long commanding tableau; a quiet passage may use short
  details. Vary duration and shot scale intentionally, not randomly.
- Every shot needs an attention plan: move, reveal, hold or cut. Stillness is an
  option, not a defect. Avoid identical zooms. Choose a coherent direction and
  supported easing/keyframes when movement helps; protect reviewed crop bounds.
  Never promise acting, lip-sync or animated weather from a camera transform.
- Match image complexity to reading time: short images read instantly; medium
  images carry an action/relationship; long images earn time through depth,
  composition, emotional weight or a staged reveal. Plan enough native crop
  detail within approved model/size constraints; no automatic size/model upgrade.
- Design and review runs of shots: scale rhythm, visual matches, screen direction,
  continuity, density, repetition, contrast and escalation. Judge the strongest
  image's placement, changed motifs and opening/closing relationship. Not every
  song needs its fastest cutting or strongest image in the last chorus.
- Transitions serve meaning. Clean cuts are a useful default; blends and other
  supported transitions need a reason. No decorative wipe quota, flashes,
  strobing or automatic beat pulses. Unsupported effects fail, never silently
  substitute. Depth/parallax is an optional selective test, not an installed
  capability or automatic upgrade: occlusion, edges, faces and hands can fail.
- Grade/grain can unify an already coherent world, not excuse face, wardrobe,
  lighting or prop drift. Scope, test and approve them; no blanket LUT or grain
  on every film. No automatic 3–4 paid candidates per shot; use deliberate proofs,
  selected references, reuse and bounded diagnosed corrections.
- Illustrate lyrics sparingly. Closed-mouth/decisive performance portraits are
  not lip-sync. Caption boxes, display text and panel reveals belong to early
  composition/readability planning, with exact lettering composited locally
  unless explicitly overridden. Typography must add meaning, not narrate every image.

## Bounded ownership and review

| Role | Music-first responsibility |
| --- | --- |
| film-director | Own the complete recording-to-sequence relationship, scope and gates; no mandatory plot. |
| music-editor | Supply evidenced timing opportunities, alternatives and uncertainty; no automatic beat snapping. |
| short-film-writer | Draft conceits/sequence arcs; causal spine for narrative directions only. |
| visual-director | Design the world and readable shot runs from early music/post input, not isolated panels. |
| post-editor | Co-design rhythm, contrast, attention plans and text before lock; later refine approved execution. |
| visual-critic | Judge the chosen mode and neighboring shots; abstraction is not rejected for lacking a protagonist. |
| film-finisher | Execute the approved sequence faithfully; no invented retiming/effects or silent fallback. |

Keep the existing two feedback lanes and one owner revision. Story/concept review
assesses narrative causality only where applicable; otherwise visual progression,
coherence and the intended musical relationship. No new committee or approval stage.

Before full image rollout, use a representative animatic on the actual song:
include dense and contrasting passages, planned text/attention mechanics and
clear placeholders for absent images. Preserve the existing proof-only full-song
preview and its approval; an excerpt alone never replaces that gate. Reuse the
three-shot proof economically, and ask before any additional paid scope.

Sequence QC: repetitive durations/framing/moves, unreadable short panels/text,
unmotivated cuts, drift, generic AI gloss, literal lyric overload, flat escalation
and decorative effects. A clean preflight or a beautiful still does not pass a
sequence. Full playback with sound remains distinct from samples and signal tests;
claim only actual coverage, and keep approval/spending user-owned.
