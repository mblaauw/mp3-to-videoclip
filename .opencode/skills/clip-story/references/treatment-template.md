# Treatment template (existing brief fields only)

Editable planning text. This is **not** a new artifact or schema — it fills
`brief.treatment` and `brief.creative.arc`, and the existing brief approval gates
it. Never invent a new formal stage for it.

## 1. Draft 2–3 directions

For each direction write a short, concrete paragraph and then the spine:

```text
Direction A — <one-line intent>
Why it fits the music: <reviewed event/phrase it rides>
Visual short story (250–400 words, narrative directions): <the actual story in
  visual prose — goal, conflict, visible actions, the choice and its cost,
  escalation, the earned ending. Open active for high-energy intent. Beat sheet
  here, not a shot list.>
Central question:
Goal or process:
Obstacle:
Choice and cost:
Turn:
Payoff:
World/locations (few, coherent):
Recurring motif and how its meaning evolves:
Risk this direction carries: <the creative risk the user should weigh>
```

For symbolic/process directions replace the short-story paragraph with the
evolving visual relationship the film follows; the spine fields still apply (a
process film's "goal" is the process, its "obstacle" the friction in it).

Offer variety, not three versions of one idea: for example (a) a protagonist
narrative, (b) a **process** film with no protagonist, (c) a symbolic/abstract
direction. Only propose directions the reviewed music can actually carry.

## 2. Example spine (illustrative, not a template to copy)

```text
Direction B — a silent lighthouse becomes a beacon again (no protagonist)
Central question: will the light reach the stranded boat before darkness?
Goal or process: follow power travelling from a failing generator to the lamp.
Obstacle: each attempted surge trips the corroded circuit before the lens turns.
Choice and cost: the final surge illuminates the lens as the building goes dark.
Turn: what looked like another failure becomes one clear sweep across the water.
Payoff: the boat turns toward the brief sweep; the dark lighthouse has mattered.
```

## 3. Record the decision

Before approval, outline a small causal chain of story beats: because A happens,
B becomes necessary; B changes what C means. Each beat must introduce information,
pressure, a decision, a consequence or an earned release. Do not map one beat to
one generated image: a beat may need several complementary stills or one hold.
Run one concise story critique (not a committee): identify the weakest causal
link, whether the turning point is visible without explanatory captions, and
whether the closing image changes the meaning of the opening. Improve that
specific weakness before buying pictures. Symbolic/abstract films use evolving
visual relationships rather than inventing a conventional protagonist/plot.

- Fill `brief.treatment` with the **user's selected** direction (their words plus
  the spine). Keep rejected directions in a draft note if useful; do not present
  your preferred direction as the selection.
- Fill `creative.arc` with the intended progression (scale, intimacy, light,
  motif evolution) mapped to reviewed events.
- Put medium/palette/light/texture in `creative.look` only; keep occupancy,
  people and action for the shot prompts.
- Then request explicit brief approval. Under explicit delegation, label the
  result **agent-reviewed under delegation** and get the actual scope/cap.

## Guardrails

- Do not invent lyrics, genre, language or emotional meaning to justify a
  direction. Supplied lyric/style text is intent, not measured evidence.
- Adults only when adult content is requested; never imitate an unapproved real
  person. A working title is not a brand, and a brand is not inferred from a
  filename or account.
- One central thread and a small set of locations beat a tour of unrelated
  worlds. Reserve one genuine visual peak.
