# Structure: Five beats

Hook, world, develop, reveal, payoff, at 8, 27, 40, 30 and 15 seconds of a two-minute film.
The hook's first image comes before the first word. The world says where we are and who is
speaking. Develop complicates what the hook promised, and is the longest beat. The reveal is one
sentence and a held image. The payoff leaves one thing behind and does not explain it.
