## Worked example

Good, in a fictional world: `{"id": "l001", "beat": "hook", "text": "This is a key. We have
never found the door.", "target_seconds": 4.5, "pause_after": 1.0, "direction": "dry, unhurried",
"claims": [{"text": "No door has been found for it", "invented": true}]}`. It is short enough to
say in one breath, it makes the viewer want the next line, and its invention is listed.

Counter-example: "In this fascinating exploration, we will discover how keys changed our world
forever." It announces instead of telling, it can't be pictured, and "changed our world forever"
is a claim nobody can check.
