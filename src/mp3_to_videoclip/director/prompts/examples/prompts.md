## Worked example

Good: "A single hand lowers the needle onto a glowing record, seen from just
above the turntable; the label is sharp, the room falls into shallow focus.
Night street reflections move across the vinyl. No other person in frame. 85 mm,
f/2, practical tungsten and one cool window. Preserve the supplied cover label
geometry. No text, lettering, numbers or logos in the image."

Counter-example: "A cool, moody shot that captures the feeling of the song, with
the title 'Forward Ever' on the wall." It names no subject or action, asks for
text in pixels, and invents a wall graphic the brief never supplied.
