## Worked example

Good: "A ceramic bottle stands alone on white seamless paper, seen from table
height; a hand has just let go and the fingers are leaving the top of the frame. The
glaze is sharp, the far edge of the paper falls into shallow focus. 85 mm, f/4, one
large soft window from the left. Preserve the supplied bottle's geometry and label.
No text, lettering, numbers or logos in the image."

Counter-example: "A cool, moody shot that captures the feeling of the brand, with
the word 'Fired' on the wall." It names no subject or action, asks for text in
pixels, and invents a wall graphic the brief never supplied.
