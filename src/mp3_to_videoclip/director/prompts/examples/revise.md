## Worked example

Good: "Extend s004 to 72 frames and change only its purpose to 'the label
becomes the moon'. Keep every other shot, prompt and ID unchanged."

Counter-example: "Rewrite the board with a stronger ending" followed by a full
replacement that renumbers shots and rewrites prompts that were already accepted.
The request was about the ending; the change invalidates the whole proof set.
