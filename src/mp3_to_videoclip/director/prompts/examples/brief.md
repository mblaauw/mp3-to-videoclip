## Worked example

Good: objective "pre-save the single 'Forward Ever' before 14 Nov"; audience
"existing listeners, 18–34, on YouTube"; message "The new single is out 14 Nov
— pre-save now"; reasons are the two facts the user gave; CTA "Pre-save now"
with the supplied URL.

Counter-example: objective "make people feel something"; audience "everyone";
message "the most exciting release of the year". It invents a superlative, names
no action, and could describe any song.
