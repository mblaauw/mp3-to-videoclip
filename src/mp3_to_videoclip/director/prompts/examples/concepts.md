## Worked example

Good: three routes for a slow harvest song. (1) Abstract: colour and rhythm are the
subject; one field of ochre narrows to a line and breaks open on each chorus.
(2) Narrative: in full daylight a family brings in a last crop and leaves one row
standing for someone who has not come home. (3) Performance: the singer works
through one take in a barn, seen only from the doorway. Each has a different shape
and a different peak.

Good beat: `{"start": 96.0, "end": 128.5, "label": "The row left standing",
"text": "The family stops at the last row as the chorus opens; nobody cuts it."}`.
It is placed on a measured section and says what changes.

Counter-example: "moody city", "warm 16 mm city" and "blue city". Same route,
three grades. The structure never changes, so the user is choosing a filter, not
a film.
