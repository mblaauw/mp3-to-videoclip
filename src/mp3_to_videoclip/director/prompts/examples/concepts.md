## Worked example

Good: three routes for a nocturnal release. (1) Narrative: a courier carries a
record across a sleeping city and arrives after the last train. (2) Performance:
the artist sings in three practical locations, each lit by one source, while the
camera stays outside the room. (3) Symbolic: a single street lamp turns on and
off across the song, changing what it illuminates. Each has a different shape and
a different peak.

Good beat: `{"start": 96.0, "end": 128.5, "label": "The last train leaves",
"text": "The courier reaches the platform as the chorus opens; the doors close on
the final bar."}`. It is placed on a measured section and says what changes.

Counter-example: "moody city", "warm 16 mm city" and "blue city". Same route,
three grades. The structure never changes, so the user is choosing a filter, not
a film.
