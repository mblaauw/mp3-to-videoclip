## Worked example

Good: a 30-second advert opens on a generated close-up (s001, scene, needle on
vinyl), cuts to a designed card (s002, card, "Two years of silence."), then a
generated bridge (s003, bridge, the label becomes the moon; match shape at
[0.5, 0.45]). Four scenes show the take; a card lands "Recorded live. One take."
(s008); a product shot and a scene carry the cover into a listener's hands; the
close is an end card with logo, date and CTA (s012). Twelve shots, three
non-scene kinds, every frame covered.

Counter-example: fourteen generated scenes, each a medium shot of the band in the
same room, with a title card added at the end. Nothing changes scale or job, the
message arrives late, and the board buys images it does not need.
