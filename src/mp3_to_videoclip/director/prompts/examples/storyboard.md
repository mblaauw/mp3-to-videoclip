## Worked example

Good: a 30-second advert opens on a generated product shot (s001, product, the
ceramic bottle on seamless paper), cuts to a designed card (s002, card, "Fired
twice."), then a generated bridge (s003, bridge, the bottle's shoulder becomes its
shadow; match shape at [0.5, 0.45]). Four scenes show the pour; a card lands "Made
by hand." (s008); a product shot and a scene carry it to a table; the close is an
end card with logo, date and CTA (s012). Twelve shots, four kinds, every frame
covered.

Counter-example: fourteen generated scenes, each a medium shot of the bottle on
the same table, with a title card added at the end. Nothing changes scale or job,
the message arrives late, and the board buys images it does not need.
