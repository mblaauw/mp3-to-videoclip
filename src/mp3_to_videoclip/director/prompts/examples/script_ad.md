## Worked example

Good: 0–3 s, a hand puts a scratched record on a turntable; text "Two years of
silence." 3–10 s, the same round label fills the frame, then becomes a moon over
a rooftop; the band is recording in one room. 10–24 s, four angles of the take,
then the cover art in a listener's hands. 24–30 s, logo, "Out 14 Nov — Pre-save
now", supplied URL. Every fact comes from the brief; the end card holds six
seconds.

Counter-example: a montage of smiling people with the line "The best album of
the decade" and no release date. The superlative is invented, the product arrives
late, and there is no single action.
