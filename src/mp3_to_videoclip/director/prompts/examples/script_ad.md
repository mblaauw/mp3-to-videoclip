## Worked example

Good: 0–3 s, a ceramic bottle stands on white seamless paper; text "Fired twice."
3–10 s, a slow turn reveals the glaze; a hand lifts the bottle and its shadow stays
a moment on the paper. 10–24 s, four angles of the pour, then the bottle back on the
paper, wet. 24–30 s, logo, "Out 14 Nov — Order now", supplied URL. Every fact comes
from the brief; the end card holds six seconds.

Counter-example: a montage of smiling people with the line "The best bottle of
the decade" and no release date. The superlative is invented, the product arrives
late, and there is no single action.
