# Music video

A music video is a visual performance of the song. The track owns the structure:
its sections decide where the film opens up, holds, cuts and lands. Meaning can
come from the song, so do not force a sales message, a product or a spoken
argument into it.

Structures that work: narrative (a small story with a turn), performance
(the artist or band carrying the frame), concept/symbolic (one visual idea that
evolves), process (how something is made), lyric-led (selected lines illustrated,
never every line), and abstract (rhythm, colour and motion as the subject).

Keep generated scenes dominant. On-screen text is rare: the title, the artist,
and a few selected lyric hits. Pacing follows the song; a held shot is earned by
the music and by depth in the frame. The default opening/closing package is the
25-second opening plus 40-second credits, editable in settings. Music videos keep
that signature requirement unless the owner changes it.
