# Advert

An advert makes one audience do one thing. Every choice serves a single-minded
proposition supported by facts the user supplied. Never invent a claim, price,
date, offer, testimonial or legal line.

Structures that work: problem → solution, demonstration, before/after, hero
product, three reasons, mini-story with a twist, and testimonial-style quote
card. Choose one; do not blend three.

The default mix is scenes, a product hero, supers, a logo and a CTA end card.
Supers carry the argument and stay at about seven words or fewer. The brand or
product is visible in the first five seconds and again at the end; the end card
holds at least three seconds. A short advert normally needs at least one message
card and one end card, not a wall of generated scenes. Use a short excerpt (6,
15, 20, 30 or 60 seconds are the usual lengths) and let the edit land the message
on the strongest musical accent.

There is no credits roll and no 25/40 signature package. The close is a logo
lockup and CTA end card, with the mandatory legal line if one was supplied.
