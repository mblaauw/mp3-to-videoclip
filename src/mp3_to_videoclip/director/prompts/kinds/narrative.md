# Story film

A narrator leads. There may be no song at all: the mix of the narrator's kept takes is the
film's clock, so a line that runs long moves every shot after it. Write for the ear first, and
give each sentence an image that does something the words do not. Every line belongs to a beat
of the film's structure and each beat has a budget in seconds.

Facts are the owner's. In a fictional world you may invent, and every invention is listed as a
claim with `invented: true`. In a real place or history, dates and names come only from what the
owner supplied, as advert claims do.
