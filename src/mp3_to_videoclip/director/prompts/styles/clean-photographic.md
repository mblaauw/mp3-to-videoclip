A clean, contemporary photograph made with a real camera: natural or motivated
light, believable colour and exposure, accurate materials and skin with visible
texture rather than a smoothed surface. Compose with the shot's lens, aperture
and focus plane so depth and subject separation come from optics, not from
uniform outlines or cut-out layers. Keep highlights controlled and shadows open
enough to read the subject. The result should feel observed on location or on a
working set, never like a 3D render, a stock-photo pose or a heavy filter.
