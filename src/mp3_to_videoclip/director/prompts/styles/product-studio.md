A controlled product-studio photograph: the product is the hero, lit with a
large soft key, a defined edge light and a clean fill so its shape, material and
label read exactly as supplied. Use a seamless surface and a restrained
background that support the product rather than compete with it. Reflections and
contact shadows are physically believable and placed deliberately. The camera is
level and precise, with a focal length that keeps the product's proportions
honest; every label edge, seam and texture stays crisp and undistorted. The
frame feels like a professional packshot, not a glossy 3D mock-up.
