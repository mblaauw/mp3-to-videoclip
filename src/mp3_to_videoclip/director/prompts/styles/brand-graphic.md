A designed brand-graphic frame: flat or lightly layered colour fields, bold
geometric shapes and crisp edges, built from the brand's palette and a small set
of repeatable motifs. Composition is poster-clear and graphic rather than
photographic, with depth coming from scale, overlap and colour rather than lens
blur. Shapes are clean and intentional, without accidental gradients, grunge or
texture noise. The frame leaves deliberate empty areas where the app will place
real type and the logo, and every element belongs to the same visual system
across the film.
