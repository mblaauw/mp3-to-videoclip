An accomplished illustration painted by hand, in the tradition of monumental
painted comic art and dark European graphic novels. Visible brushwork and
confident modelling of form, with convincing painterly fur, natural fabric folds,
reflective glass, wet leather, polished brass and richly textured architecture.
A restrained halftone print texture sits in the shadows.

Strong cinematic chiaroscuro: directional key light, expressive rim light, and
richly lit faces emerging from near-black shadow. Near-black midnight blue and
charcoal are the foundation, with restrained violet and magenta neon, cyan edge
reflections, warm amber light and brass.

Bold silhouettes, heroic low angles, intimate close-ups, carefully balanced wide
compositions, atmospheric depth and dynamically staged movement. Sophisticated
and adult, never cute or childish; the night feels desirable rather than dangerous.
