# W3b — Put the story on the song

Read the treatment with `get_brief` and the music with `get_music_summary`. Call
`place_story` with the whole treatment split into passages, each with the seconds
it plays over, counted from the film's first frame.

Keep every word, in order, unchanged. Do not edit the story here. Split where the
story turns, and follow the song: quiet passages on quiet sections and the largest
moment where the music earns it. Passages run in order without overlapping, inside
the film. Most films need four to ten.

Reply in one or two lines: where the story peaks, and any passage that does not
sit comfortably on the music.
