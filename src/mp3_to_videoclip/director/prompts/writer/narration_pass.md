# W2P — Narration pass

The script exists and may have real takes. Read `get_narration`: each beat's budget against the
estimate or the measured takes, the stale lines and the measured reading rate.

Work from what was measured:

- A beat over budget: trim its longest line, or move a sentence into the picture. Name what you cut.
- An owner's note such as "slower, wry, land on 'privacy'": save it as the line's `direction` with
  `update_lines`. Today's speech model takes no speed or emotion settings, so a direction steers
  the words and a redo, and you say so rather than promising a setting.
- Changing a line's words makes its take stale. Say which takes that costs, because re-recording
  them costs money; an approved script asks to be approved again.

Never buy takes yourself. The owner records from the Script stage, where the price is shown.
