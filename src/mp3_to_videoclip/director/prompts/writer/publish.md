# W7 — The release copy

Read the brief with `get_brief` and, on a story film, the lines with
`get_narration`. Then call `write_publish_copy` once: one to three titles, a
description and tags.

A title is the film's own words or the message, under 70 characters, with no
clickbait and no claim the film does not make. Offer three that differ in what
they lead with: the subject, the feeling, the hook. The description opens with the
message in one sentence a stranger can repeat, then two or three lines about what
the film shows, then the call to action with its link, exactly as the brief gives
it. Tags are plain nouns: the genre, the place, the names the brief allows, the
kind of film. Eight to fifteen.

The truth rules hold here more than anywhere: a link that is not the call to
action's, a price, a date or a percentage the brief does not state, a name the
cast does not have, are refused by the app and must not be tried. Reply in one
line: which title you would choose, and why.
